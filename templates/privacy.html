Privacy

What happens when you join the waitlist, in plain words.

What we keep

  • Your email address.
  • When you joined, and when you confirmed.
  • Where you came from, if the link you followed said so. For example, a link from a newsletter.
  • A scrambled code made from your IP address. We use it only to slow down abuse. We never store the address itself.

Why

To tell you when you can try Dijmo. We don’t sell your email or share it for marketing.

Who else sees it

Our email provider, Resend, delivers our emails. Our own server stores the list. No one else.

What we don’t do

  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
  • Our emails have no tracking pixels.
  • If you turn the sound on, your browser remembers that on your device. It is never sent to us.

How long we keep it

Until you leave. If you don’t confirm, the link in your email stops working after 72 hours, and we delete the signup after 30 days.

How to leave

Every email we send has a link to leave the waitlist. One click removes your email.

Questions

Reply to any email from us, and a person will answer.
