Dijmo, four ways

Four directions for the new logo. Each has a mark, a wordmark, a macOS icon and a favicon, in light, dark and one colour. Every letter is an outline cut from Schibsted Grotesk, the site’s own face, so nothing depends on an installed font.

Caret

The d’s stem is the caret: cut free of its bowl, red, and running a little under the baseline the way a playhead crosses a track.

Weak spot. A letter with one coloured stroke is a familiar monogram move, so the idea lives in the colour and the cut more than in the silhouette.

Cut

Weak spot. Split circles are common in logos, and this one says record and cut rather than words; in the wordmark the o reads as a dot first.

Thock

A porcelain keycap whose legend is the caret: the site’s thock made into an object, and at home on a gamer’s desk.

Weak spot. It says keyboard before video, and without the material icon the flat mark is a rounded square that many apps could own.

My pick: Caret

It is the site’s own idea, the caret is the playhead, made into a letter. It is the quietest of the four, the clearest at 16 pixels, and it keeps its meaning in one colour because the cut, not the red, separates caret from bowl.

Cut is the boldest in the Dock and would make a good recording indicator inside the app whichever direction wins. Files, sizes and how to rebuild them are in README.md.
